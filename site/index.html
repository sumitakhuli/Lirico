[00:42.00] the hard part

Music videos start with an intro. Lirico waits for the singer.

Lyrics are usually timed to the album track, so on a music video they run early. Lirico listens to what your PC plays, hears the first sung words, and lines everything up from there. The listening happens on your PC — nothing is recorded, saved or sent.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

That’s SmartScreen being careful with a new app. Click More info → Run anyway. Lirico then lives in your system tray.

Where are my settings?

Right-click the tray icon → Settings for fonts, colours, the underline, hotkeys and sync. Everything is saved in your user folder under .lirico.

A song shows no lyrics

Lirico only shows lyrics it can find with timing. Some songs aren’t in the free database yet — you’ll see “No synced lyrics found” and it stays out of your way.
